The house

A guesthouse in a hilltop village, run by Sergey and Vinod

OmiHomeStay is a 10-room guesthouse in Upper Bhagsu, above McLeod Ganj, wrapped in Himalayan cedar forest — right between Bhagsu and Dharamkot, about 10 minutes on foot to either. Every room has a desk, a kettle with tea and coffee and a hot shower; most look out at the mountains. Omi Home Cafe cooks North Indian, Indo-Chinese and continental dishes, daily 07:00–21:00.

07 — Good to know

Questions,
answered

Check-in & check-out

Check-in 14:00–21:00. Arriving after 21:00? Let us know your arrival time in advance (call or WhatsApp) — once we know it, we leave the key in your room door and send you the room number. Check-out 11:00–12:00.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates, number of guests and preferred room — we confirm availability and the best direct price right away. You can also book on Booking.com or Airbnb.

Is breakfast included?

No — the on-site café runs separately and cooks to order (Indian & international, paid). Staying guests get 10% off the menu. Every room has a kettle with a tea & coffee kit.

Heating? AC?

Winters are cold: heating is available in every room for a fee. No AC — at ~1,800 m you won't miss it; summer nights are cool.

Stairs, parking, arrival

The house has stairs only (no elevator) — rooms are on floors 2–4. Motorbikes and scooters can stay at the house; no car parking on the property — free street parking nearby, few spots. We'll gladly arrange an airport taxi (paid to the driver).

Documents

Every guest shows ID at check-in: foreign guests passport + visa (C-form is mandatory), Indian guests a government photo ID.

House rules

No pets, no parties. Smoking outside/on the terrace only, not in rooms. Quiet hours 22:00–07:00. Please leave muddy or wet shoes at the door.

Long stays

Weekly and monthly guests are very welcome — desks, kitchens and weekly cleaning make it easy (paid laundry on the ground floor, about ₹100/kg). Ask on WhatsApp for long-stay rates.
